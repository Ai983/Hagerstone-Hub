## Outreach mechanics (suggested)

1. **Channel:** WhatsApp-first or phone (this segment lives on WhatsApp; cold email alone underperforms). Lead with the demo: *"We built this to run our own ₹XX Cr fit-out business — 2-minute video of a PO approved on WhatsApp."*
2. **The hook per segment:** procurement firms → AI invoice/quote parsing; site-heavy firms (civil/facade/MEP) → imprest/petty-cash control on WhatsApp; furniture/signage mfrs → PO + vendor management.
3. **Track it:** move this list into a simple sheet/CRM with columns: contacted date → response → demo → decision. (The marketing schema in the Hub DB could hold this.)
4. **Caveats:** Vector Projects & Eccellenza contacts came from directories (sites down/minimal) — re-verify. Flipspaces, Officebanao, AFC, Glass Wall are above the ideal size band — treat as slower "anchor" pursuits, not first calls.
5. **Legal note:** these are publicly listed business contacts, fine for B2B outreach; keep it personalized, one-to-one (bulk-blast to scraped lists damages the WhatsApp number's standing and the brand).
